# Dev log

A dated record of what changed each day. The game itself lives in a Roblox place (scripts are not in this repo), so this log and `tools/` are the public record of the work.

## 2026-10-26
- **Phone Shadow Market:** the shop was the desktop window shrunk to postage-stamp size on phones. Added a compact phone layout (big sort/filter chips, one list, a 3D preview with BUY / gems / ROLL buttons). The Fortune roll screen was checked in phone mode and is fine. Fusion Room is still desktop-sized on phones and is next.
- Refreshed the crew store slide (bigger faces, no empty torsos). Added a Studio-only test hook (`DevOpenMenu`) for phone audits; it does nothing in the live game.
- **"Next step" on the results screen and phone fixes:** the results screen now has a NEXT STEP strip with a GO button (the same suggestion the lobby's Next Up card gives: use your free spin, claim the daily reward, take on the next realm; after a loss it suggests tuning the deck in the Hideout). New players with an unused spin are told to roll first. Phone layout: the results window is shorter (so it scales up and the text is readable) with at most 2 progress rows, and the training card moves to the top-right on touch screens because the top-left covered the ability and hero cards the lessons point at. Also redrew and uploaded the "REALM CLEARED!" title image, which still had the old word baked in. Tested in Studio's phone mode with sample results; not on a real phone.
- **First "good moment" and training analytics:** the first time a player clears wave 1 of the training run they now get one free roll (+1 spin) with a "FREE ROLL!" banner and sound, once per account (stored in their profile so repeating the training cannot farm it). Also logs which training step each new player reaches (`TutStep` event) so drop-off can be measured. Tested in Studio: spins went 35 to 36 exactly once across two cleared waves, the banner slides in, no errors. Growth plan is in `GROWTH_ROADMAP.md` (kept with the store art, outside the repo).
- **Faster first five minutes (training flow):** new players used to read about 12 interface-explanation screens before placing a tower. Reordered the 35-step training to 22 steps: a one-line goal, then place two towers, start wave 1, and the interface (hearts, yen, ability, controls) is explained in short hints during the wave instead of before it. Upgrade, extra towers, weakness lesson, overdrive, boss-interrogation practice and the ending are kept, just reordered and shortened. Tested in Studio by starting the training run and driving the first 12 steps (towers placed, wave started, hints shown, no errors); the later steps reuse the same logic but I could not press every key in Studio. Not tested with real players yet. A written plan with the targets to measure is in `FIRST_5_MINUTES_PLAN.md` (kept outside the repo with the store art).
- **Store art:** new 1920x1080 thumbnail slides (gameplay, city lobby, the crew, bosses, abilities, team up) and two icon options, plus video ideas.
- **Original characters and terms (IP clean-up before any monetization):** the nine thief characters were the Persona 5 cast and several terms were Persona-specific. Renamed the characters (Joker, Ryuji, Ann, Futaba, Morgana, Yusuke, Makoto, Haru, Akechi became Vesper, Brick, Ember, Cipher, Sable, Sumi, Rook, Lumi, Umber), replaced their Persona 5 codenames and two ability names, and rewrote the self-introductions and the cat's old codename in the bond scenes. Renamed the visible terms: Palace to Realm, Persona to Phantom, Phantom Thieves to The Crew, Velvet Rolls to Fortune Rolls, Velvet Room to Fusion Room, Confidant to Partner, All-Out to Overdrive. 331 string changes in 39 scripts plus 12 texts stored in the place. Internal IDs and saved data were deliberately left alone, so nobody's progress breaks. Checked in Studio: no errors, a test run to wave 5, and no old terms visible in the UI.
- **Character art redesign:** a side-by-side render showed the nine thief portraits matched the Persona 5 originals (colour schemes plus signature props such as the skull mask). Gave each an original look: new palettes, and new faces and props (Vesper's gold one-eye half-mask, Brick's bandana, Ember's flame ponytail and teal mask, Cipher's goggles and headphones, Sable as a slate cat with a bandana, Sumi's brush-stroke mask, Rook's visor and battlement crown, Lumi's flower pin, Umber's fedora), plus the remaining codename ability and passive names. The lobby avatar outfit code is switched off in the live game (players keep their own Roblox avatar), so those colour edits are inert.
- **New window-title lettering:** drew and uploaded six new title images in the old style (Choose a Realm, Realm Gate, Realm Scout, Partner Bonds, Fortune Rolls, Fusion Room) so those windows keep their stylised titles instead of falling back to plain text.
- **Still not covered:** other Persona 5 mechanic names (Interrogation, Shadows, Treasure, Hideout), the tarot-arcana pairing per character (changing it would change tower bonuses), other baked-in artwork I haven't inspected, and the character archetypes in the bond-scene dialogue.
- **Phone fixes (from 2026-10-25):** the phone Hideout and Palace Gate now show 3D previews with an action button (they were text-only), and `UI.button` adds an invisible larger tap area on touch devices so small buttons are easy to tap. Tested in Studio's phone mode only, not on a real phone.

## 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-25
- **In-run performance pass:** the user lagged on a Mac, so I measured render cost in Studio (triangles and draw batches, solo only, not real Mac FPS). Shadows roughly doubled the triangle count in a run, mostly from characters, towers and enemy bodies. Added a client `PerfGovernor` (world culling of the lobby, city and other palaces during a run, a light and particle budget, run-time shadow trimming, and an auto quality step-down), a GRAPHICS setting (AUTO / HIGH / BALANCED / FAST, saved with the profile), mesh quality set to Performance, and halved the lobby sky clouds and fog. Gameplay numbers were not touched. Details and rollback notes are in `PERF_CHANGES_REVIEW.md`.
- **Smoother enemy movement:** enemies arrive from the server in uneven batches, so they looked choppy on the track. The client now estimates each enemy's velocity and glides toward it; in Studio the per-frame step went from alternating about 1.4 and 2.4 studs (standard deviation 0.48) to a steady 0.48 (standard deviation 0.05). The user confirmed it is smoother on their Mac.
- **Enemy fixes:** heavy enemies (Titan, Bulwark) stood sideways because limb positions were measured after the server had already turned them; the server now sends each limb's rest position. About 75% of every enemy's parts were invisible leftovers from the old figure (Guard 101 parts down to 27, Titan 126 to 30, same visible parts); they are now deleted.
- **Effects toned down:** hit rings and kill rings are smaller and fainter, and the wave-start and wave-clear bursts are about half the size.
- **Hard mode:** added a gentler health ramp for early Hard waves and per-palace Hard easing, and eased Greed's early waves on Normal. Re-running the other palaces on Hard is still to do.
- **UI text:** smaller WEAK / RESIST labels, move-name popups and analysis panel.
- **Not verified:** FPS on a Mac or phone, multiplayer, and memory over many waves. Still to do: re-run the balance bot on Hard, test on a phone, delete the test bot before the final publish.

## 2026-10-22
- **Mobile performance (the main finding of the day):** profiled a run. Each enemy is a model of about 100 anchored parts and the server moved every part every tick, so every client received roughly 100 part updates per enemy per frame. Enemies now have one anchored root with everything else welded to it (the few limbs the client animates itself stay unwelded), so the server moves one part per enemy. Checked in a run: enemies follow the path and no part is out of place. On phones only, I also turned off world shadows, cut every particle emitter to 40%, switched off lights far from the camera during a run, skipped animating parts more than 300 studs away, and detach the lobby, its cities and the other palaces while a run is on (workspace parts in a run went from about 10,500 to about 1,500; everything is re-attached on returning to the lobby and the character lands on the floor). All verified by forcing phone mode in Studio, not on a real phone.
- **Mobile layout:** the touch buttons (dash, all-out, hero, melee, cancel) used fixed pixel offsets and covered the Yen, All-Out and wave panels on small screens. They now size from the screen height and stack just above the Yen panel. On phones the wave preview card starts closed and moves to the top row, and the difficulty tag is hidden. The lobby dash button now only appears while on a parkour or skyway course, instead of sitting on the lobby UI all the time.
- **Smoother transitions:** windows shrink and fade out instead of vanishing, the lobby-to-run wipe has a white edge and halftone and a longer fade, and the loading screen shows real download progress and slides out.

## 2026-10-21
- **Balance:** found why two palaces (Despair and Doubt) could not be won even at low enemy health: their two lanes sat about 80 studs apart. Pulled the lanes together, eased their first waves, and re-tuned enemy health for every palace from Sloth to the Archipelago with the balance bot. Details and tables in `tools/balance_bot_results.md`.
- **Tutorial:** added a melee lesson and made players keep the tutorial team when they finish training. A human tester played it and said it works.
- **Daily rewards:** added a live countdown to the next reset on the Daily Rewards window.
- **Achievements:** replaced three unreachable S-rank achievements with kill-based ones (same IDs, so existing claims stay valid); added a "!" badge on the Hideout tab when something is ready to claim.
- **Mobile:** added a hold-to-swing MELEE button for phones and re-spaced the touch buttons so they fit short screens.
- **Persona previews:** previews in the hideout and market now fade in whole once their meshes load instead of popping in piece by piece.
- **Lobby look and sound:** softer tree colors and lighting, removed the large "Phantom Lounge" sign, new lobby music.
- **The Hideaway (safe room):** a large indoor lounge with a coffee-and-curry bar, a balcony and fire pit over the city skyline, working couches and booths, a stage with a jukebox, and a bartender. Players earn a slow trickle of Spins for staying, capped per day, with an idle-kick workaround so people can stay. About 750 parts, so it has little effect on performance.
- **Towers:** added three new tower types: Longshot (map-spanning sniper), Zone (lingering pools on the path) and Beam (a beam that ramps up while locked on), each with its own 3-path, 5-tier upgrade tree; seven personas moved onto them. Tier 5 is now rare: a player can have only one tier-5 copy of each persona at a time.
- **UI remake, phase 1:** new shared window, button, panel and toast pieces in an original red, black and white style (layered tilted shards, thick outlines, diagonal accents, cut-out titles) so every screen that uses them picks up the look at once. Checked on the Hideout screen. Found and fixed a bug I had introduced earlier: the new "!" badge on the Hideout Achievements tab read a property that does not exist on a button, which stopped the whole Hideout window from building.
- **Towers and heroes face their targets:** each tower and hero now turns smoothly toward what it attacks. The server only sends the aim point; each player's screen does the turn, and the animated parts (orbiting gems, wings, bobbing pieces) are tied to the turning body so nothing is left behind. Checked visually on a persona and a hero.
- **Balance pass (no items):** reran the bot through all 15 palaces with heart charms and smoke bombs switched off, using the new towers; most palaces became flawless wins and Greed and Doubt were too hard. Fixed the weak early Zone tower, retuned enemy health per palace, and added the full table to `tools/balance_bot_results.md`. Also moved three heroes onto the new tower types (artillery, frost beam, grave field).
- **Upgrade rewrite:** audited the upgrade data (54 of 77 personas had their own trees; 145 of 810 tiers were only numbers). Rewrote all 145 so every tier now changes how the tower plays (marks, burns, slows, stuns, extra targets, pierce, ricochet, kill chains, death blasts, per-wave income), with descriptions generated from the effects so text and behavior cannot drift apart. Gave the last seven shared-tree deck towers their own trees, so every deck tower now has a unique 3-path, 5-tier tree. Caught and reverted a mistake in my own change: three of the personas I had moved to the new types turned out to be heroes, so the new types now use real deck towers instead.
- **Docs:**  added `SYSTEMS.md`, a short write-up of each game system.
- **Lobby hub (six stations):** a Today board, a challenge giver, a team-finder board, a leaderboard wall, a trophy case, and a practice range that runs a free-play session with your own deck and no rewards.
- **UI remake, phase 2:** phase 1 was too timid, so I drew a small texture kit (cut-corner frame, slanted buttons, torn title banner, halftone dots, brush strokes) with a script and uploaded it. Windows, buttons and toasts now use it. The in-run HUD, hotbar cards (now showing each tower's type), speed buttons, item chips, tower panel, Palace Gate cards and persona cards got the same treatment. Deck slots in the Hideout now show the tower type too. Not yet checked on a real phone, and I never saw the tower panel's upgrade rows populated in Studio.
- **Lobby clutter and layout:** floating station names now show only when you are close; the Menu list no longer overlaps the team card and Gems/Spins chips; the Hideaway music panel moved off the bottom edge; the Hideaway door has a light pillar and a Navigator entry so it is findable.
- **Hideaway polish:** the dance floor and rugs were sitting at the same height and flickering through each other (z-fighting); fixed that and two overlapping wall corners, and changed the black and purple floor to warm wood.
- **Atmosphere:** dimmed the neon on the floor, stations and skyline, then recoloured the skyline windows (mostly white with red accents). Added lobby-only fog: denser haze plus three drifting fog banks between the towers. Made the three skyline model sets persistent so they stop streaming in and out as you walk; I could not reproduce the pop-in in Studio, so that fix is untested beyond the city loading correctly.
